## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Fix promotions, shrink and home delivery rather than cut labor: margin reaches about 2.3% by FY2028, and 3.0% only if nearly everything goes right

**Executive summary:**
1. Wages are not the main cause: gross margin and e-commerce fulfillment explain 79% of the margin decline
2. Store labor explains only 13% of the fall from 3.37% to 1.02% margin
3. Promotions and e-commerce are not earning what their teams told the board
4. 38% of FY2025 promotions lost money; e-commerce lost about $10M, mostly on delivery
5. Management's 8% labor cut would net about $8M, not $14.5M, because shrink rises where hours are thin
6. The leanest-staffed stores lose 3.1% of sales to shrink, against 2.3% in the best-staffed
7. Five moves worth about $25M a year lift margin to about 2.3% by FY2028 after the wage rise, short of 3.0%
8. Cutting promotions is worth $10.5M a year; six store exits add $4.4M
9. The labor-cut draft would leave margin near 1.3%
10. Delivery fees, promotion cuts and shrink fixes go first; the CFO should open covenant talks with the lender now
11. If the decline stops, wave 1 needs 29% of its value to clear the $20M covenant
