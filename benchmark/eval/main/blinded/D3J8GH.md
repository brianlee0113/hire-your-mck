## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** enter the US in 2027 through Amazon (Option A), reset the 2030 targets, and add specialty retail only after awareness reaches 20%

**Executive summary:**
1. The US is the right market for the 2027 slot. US K-beauty facial skincare is $3.6B and grew 17% a year in 2021–25. The Amazon pilot nearly doubled monthly units in six months at 4.5 stars. By 2030, Option A builds a US business of $26M, against $7M of extra revenue from a Japan scale-up. Japan is safer on cash to 2030, and it can follow in 2028.
2. Amazon-led (A) beats retail-led (B) on every board test. On its own inputs, B breaches the $18M cash cap (–$20.8M by 2030), does not pay back and cuts group margin to 4.5% in 2027. Its 4.5 units/door/week is what brands with >20% awareness sell (Onsoo: 4%), and its 1.3× Amazon halo rests on a correlation.
3. Even Option A will not hit every target, so the board should reset two of them. A's peak cash need is $1.9–4.9M in every case except the severe one, and group margin stays at 9% or more. But payback by 2030 needs ad cost (ACoS) of ≤28.6% against a 32% benchmark (otherwise ~2032), and 2030 revenue is $26M, not $40M ($40M around 2032).
4. Specialty retail should come later, behind stricter gates. At the CEO's 15% awareness trigger, brands sell a median 3.5 units/door/week, below the retailer's 4.0 removal line. Adding national retail in 2029 would cut 2029 group margin to 6.8% and leave US cash at –$9.6M in 2030. Gate retail on ≥20% awareness and a measured halo.
5. Decisions for March 2027: (1) Approve Option A with a $5M peak-cash envelope. (2) Restate the US targets: $25M+ revenue in 2030, $40M by ~2032, payback by 2030 at ≤28.6% ACoS and by 2032 at most. (3) Reject retail-first and replace the 2029 retail trigger with evidence gates. (4) Review the Japan scale-up for 2028; the bandwidth limit applies to 2027 only.
