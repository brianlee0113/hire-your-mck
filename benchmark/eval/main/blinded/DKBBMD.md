## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Yes, raise prices in 2027 — but by segment: mid-market and enterprise from January, SMB only after a test

**Executive summary:**
1. Churn is rising in one place: SMB monthly logo churn rose 2.0% → 3.1%; mid-market 0.6% → 0.8%; enterprise flat at 0.2%. Within SMB, the 2025 50%-off promotion cohort explains ~90% of the increase. Other SMB customers: 2.0% → 2.1%.
2. Price matters — but not where the CFO plan applies it hardest: Core SMB churners cite price no more than in 2025 (18% vs 19%). But promo churners do (52%) after their discount ended, and Inkwell’s $9 Starter has cut our contested SMB win rate from 51% to 38%.
3. $10.3M is a headline, not a 2027 number: 12% × all ARR ignores renewal timing and enterprise caps: only $7.6M is capturable in 2027, and an SMB churn response cuts that to ~$5.0–6.3M, losing ~320–640 SMB customers.
4. Recommendation: Mid-market +10% at renewal ($3.5M) · Enterprise to contract terms — 7% where capped, 10% otherwise ($0.8M) · Hold SMB list prices in H1, run a controlled renewal test and an activation programme. = $4.3M, up to ~$5.1M if the April/July checkpoints pass.
5. What it delivers: If segments repeat trailing NRR, ARR reaches ~$98.3M (+14.2%); the 15% goal ($98.9M) needs ≥$0.7M new-logo ARR or the checkpoint upside. NRR stays ~114%, far above the 100% floor.
