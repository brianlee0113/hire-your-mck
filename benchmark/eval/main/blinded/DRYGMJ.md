## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Enter the US in 2027 through Amazon, not national retail, and hold ad costs near the pilot's 28% so it pays back by 2030

**Executive summary:**
1. The US is worth entering: K-beauty skincare nearly doubled to $3.6B, and Onsoo's Amazon test sells (61% of K-beauty buyers buy mostly on Amazon; Pilot sales nearly doubled in six months)
2. Amazon-led entry fits the cash cap easily but pays back by 2030 only if ad costs stay near the pilot's (Payback needs ACoS of 28.6% or less; About $26M of revenue in 2030, not $40M)
3. Retail-led entry fails the board's tests even on the planning team's own inputs (Newcomers like Onsoo sell 2.6 units a door a week; Cash outflow passes the $18M cap in 2029)
4. A Japan scale-up is safer but adds only $7M of revenue in 2030, in a slow market (Japan pays back in 2029, with $1.8M by 2030)
5. Launch on Amazon in 2027 under an ad-cost ceiling; add retail only once awareness passes 20% (Ambition restated: about $26M by 2030)
