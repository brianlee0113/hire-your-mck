## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Raise prices on mid-market and enterprise renewals in 2027; hold SMB prices until a test shows a rise is safe

**Executive summary:**
1. Raise prices on mid-market and enterprise renewals in 2027; hold SMB prices until a test shows a rise is safe
2. Rising churn comes from the 2025 promotion cohort, not from a reaction to list prices
3. About 90% of the rise in lost customers is promotion customers
4. Fixing the promotion cohort cuts SMB churn by a third, not by half
5. Larger customers should carry a 10% rise, checked against a renewal control group
6. A 10% rise left mid-market conversion at 23.1% against 23.6%
7. SMB is where a rise could backfire: Inkwell sells at $9 against our $15
8. Win rate in SMB deals with Inkwell fell from 51% to 38% in a year
9. A 12% rise stops paying at twice the 2023 churn response
10. A segmented rise adds about $4.3M and can reach 15% growth with little new business
11. The CFO's $10.3M is about $6.3M after timing, caps and churn
12. Only $0.6M of new ARR is needed; NRR rises to about 114%
13. Reprice larger renewals in January, test SMB first, and re-activate the cohort
14. Decide SMB prices in July against the 2023 response
