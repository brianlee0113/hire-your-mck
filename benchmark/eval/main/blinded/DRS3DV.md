## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Launch Dispatch Copilot in a new Pro tier at +$12 per seat in Q4 2027, not as a $149 add-on in Q2: it is a ~$2M ARR expansion lever, and the mobile rewrite should go first

**Executive summary:**
1. Copilot is worth launching, but it is worth ~$2M of ARR, not $8M. The $8M rests on a survey with a 6% response rate; only 14% of engaged beta accounts actually paid $149. Realistic new ARR is $1.2–2.9M. It is still the only lever in the pack that adds the expansion NRR needs: without it NRR reaches ~103%, short of 105%.
2. Package it in Pro at +$12 per seat with a usage allowance, not as a $149 add-on. A flat per-account fee draws the heaviest users (buyers average 6,800 requests, $61 a month of inference) and is an extra charge where Routewise includes AI in Pro. Pro base case: $2.2M ARR, $0.9M gross profit, 76.6% company margin. Free inclusion would cut margin to 70–74%.
3. Launch in Q4 2027 and keep the mobile rewrite on schedule. Mobile reliability causes 27% of churn, AI 6%. A two-quarter mobile delay loses ~$0.54M of ARR for good; a two-quarter Copilot delay loses ~$0.28M and defers ~$0.4M of profit. The money is close, so the stronger evidence and longer-lasting effect of the mobile fix decide it.
4. Use 2027 to prove Copilot with non-volunteers, with a trigger to launch sooner. Run a randomised Pro pilot, keep beta accounts live, equip Sales for Routewise deals and renegotiate inference before Dec 2027. Bring launch forward to Q2 only if the Q4 2026 data show AI, not Routewise's 20% discount, drove the lost deals and the rewrite will fix <40% of mobile churn.
