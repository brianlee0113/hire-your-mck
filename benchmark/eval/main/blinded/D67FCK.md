## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Raise prices in 2027 in mid-market and enterprise only; hold SMB until a test supports it, and treat churn as a promotion-cohort problem

**Executive summary:**
1. Recommendation: from January 2027, raise mid-market and enterprise prices +10% at renewal (7% where enterprise contracts cap increases). Hold SMB list prices and test +8% in H1. This adds ~$3.5M ARR ($2.4M stress case), keeps NRR at ~113%, and leaves ~$1.5M of new-customer ARR needed to reach the +15% target, against $4.9M with no increase.
2. 1 Rising churn comes from the 2025 promotion cohort; the core base has not become more price-sensitive
3. Non-promotion SMB churn: 2.0% → 2.1% a month. Mid-market and enterprise are ~flat.
4. The 2,100 promotion customers churn at ~4.9% a month. They explain ~1.0pp of the 1.1pp SMB rise.
5. Price as the exit reason: 18% of non-promotion churners (19% last year) vs 52% of promotion churners.
6. Onboarding helps (~$0.4M) but cannot halve SMB churn as Customer Success expects.
7. 2 Pricing power sits in mid-market and enterprise (76% of ARR); SMB is exposed to Inkwell
8. MM/Ent: NRR 112–118%, churn ≤0.8% a month; customers stay for integrations and the audit trail.
9. A +10% MM list test showed no significant effect on conversion (23.1% vs 23.6%).
10. SMB: Inkwell is $9 against our $15, and SMB win rate where Inkwell competed fell from 51% to 38%.
11. A +12% SMB increase is worth ~$1.25M at the 2023 price response, and ~$0 if sensitivity has doubled.
12. 3 The CFO's 12% plan is worth ~$5.3M, not $10.3M; the segmented plan trades ~$1.8M of upside for less risk
13. Only 2027 renewals can be repriced, and caps apply: $10.3M falls to $7.6M before any churn reaction.
14. After churn reaction: $5.3M base case, $2.7M stress case. Most of the gap is SMB.
15. Segmented plan: $3.5M base, $2.4M stress, with no SMB downside and within the tested price level.
16. The SMB test can add $0.3–0.7M from July if it passes the breakeven rules.

**Recommendation:** From January 2027, raise mid-market and enterprise prices +10% at renewal (7% where enterprise contracts cap increases). Hold SMB list prices and test +8% in H1 (randomised test on new self-serve sign-ups, go to +8% from July if conversion falls < 7% relative and win rate holds). Reject the uniform 12%.
