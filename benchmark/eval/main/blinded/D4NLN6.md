## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Raise prices in 2027 where customers stay: 12% for mid-market, enterprise up to its caps, and SMB only after a test

**Executive summary:**
1. Rising churn comes from 2025 promotion customers reaching full price: they explain 91% of the SMB rise – SMB churn outside the promotion moved only from 2.0% to 2.1% a month (p. 5–10)
2. Mid-market has shown it will pay more and enterprise rarely leaves, but SMB pricing is unproven with Inkwell at $9 – Our SMB win rate against Inkwell fell from 51% to 38% in a year (p. 12–16)
3. The CFO's 12% can add only $7.6M in 2027, and mid-market and enterprise rises alone clear the $98.9M target – Without any SMB rise, 2027 ARR still reaches about $99.03M (p. 18–23)
4. Activation is the lever on the promotion cohort's churn, but fixing it cuts SMB churn by up to a third, not half – Only 41% of promotion customers activated in month one, against 72% elsewhere (p. 25–28)
5. Approve in December, raise mid-market and enterprise from January, and decide SMB in July – A Q1 test of +8% on non-promotion SMB renewals gates the July decision (p. 30–34)
