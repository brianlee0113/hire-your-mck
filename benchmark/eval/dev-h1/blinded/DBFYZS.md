## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Do not launch Parcelbird Now in 2027; invest in the software business, which can reach profitability within current cash

**Executive summary:**
1. The market is growing fast, but no operator makes money in it
2. The launch case needs six conditions to hold, and the evidence supports none of them
3. GMV grew 42% a year in 2022–25, yet both incumbents lose money on every order and one is retreating
4. A dark store would lose money on every order, and customers leave before repaying their acquisition cost
5. It loses $6.17 per order at the median and $1.76 at best observed; only a plan beyond any real site profits
6. Our pop-up lost $12.57 per order, and only 22% of its customers were still ordering in month 3
7. Even on the founders' own numbers, the launch breaks the investors' cash constraints
8. It needs $9.5M against $7.2M spendable above the runway floor; with core burn, cash runs out in month 13
9. The software business is a better use of the same cash
10. It breaks even in about month 26 at 60% growth and keeps 12 months of runway while growth stays at 34%+
11. Decline the launch, back the software plan with phased hires, and set triggers before any re-entry
12. Approve $0.8M a year of hires in tranches, build a store-based offer for grocers, track four triggers
