**Governing thought:** Parcelbird should not launch Parcelbird Now in 2027; it should put the cash into its software and reopen quick commerce only if customer sites prove it pays

**Executive summary:**
1. Quick commerce is growing fast, but no operator has yet made it profitable
2. US sales grew 2.8 times in three years, to $5.1B in 2025
3. Both incumbents lose money per order; QuickBasket closed 4 of 11 cities
4. At the economics of Parcelbird's own customer sites every order loses money, and pricing does not close the gap
5. At the median customer site, each order would lose $6.17

10. Scaling the software is the better use of cash; reopen quick commerce only when gates are met
11. Software hires use 11% of cash above the floor; the launch needs 132%
12. Nine late-stage deals would add about $0.9M of ARR, above the $0.8M hires' cost
