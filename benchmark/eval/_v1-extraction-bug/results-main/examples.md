## Substantive reasoning errors in a Hire Your McK deck: c3-ai-assistant, Hire Your McK, replicate 2

Weighted 4.05; 2 numerical errors, 2 contradictions, 5 unsupported claims (primary judge).

- Numerical error: "AI features, 6%: fifth of six reasons, behind price and missing integrations" — judge: Sixth (last) of the six reasons in Table 3; 'Other' at 16% and 'Went out of business / sold' at 14% also rank above it.
- Numerical error: "$12 per seat a month ÷ $0.01 per request = 1,333.3 requests per seat a month" — judge: The divisor is $0.009 per request ($12 ÷ $0.009 = 1,333); at $0.01 the result would be 1,200. The result is right but the displayed input is rounded wrongly.
- Contradiction: "A pooled allowance of about 1,330 requests per seat keeps Pro's AI cost below the $12 each seat pays ... Pro with allowance: 76.0%" vs "Pro without allowance: if Pro attracts users as heavy as the paywall buyers, margin falls to 74.1% ... Pro without allowance uses 6,800 requests a month per account" — judge: 6,800 requests on a 6.2-seat account is about 1,100 per seat, inside the 1,333 allowance (about 8,270 per account). The proposed allowance would not stop the 74.1% scenario, so the 76.0% 'with allowance' figure is really just the mean-usage case.
- Contradiction: "Even 14% is a ceiling: the buyers came from volunteers, who are larger and more active than the base" vs "Pro earns more ARR, even against the add-on's ceiling ... $2.9M Pro ARR at 33% take-up" — judge: Paid demand evidence is discounted for the add-on, but an untested 33% precedent from a different tier is accepted for Pro. The document's own backup shows Pro at 14% earns $1.2M, below the add-on's $2.45M, so the revenue ranking is not robust.

## Substantive reasoning errors in a baseline deck: c1-market-entry, Strong-baseline Claude, replicate 3

Weighted 4.40; 2 numerical errors, 2 contradictions, 3 unsupported claims (primary judge).

- Numerical error: "Japan is ahead on cash to 2030 unless ACoS stays at or below the pilot's 28%" — judge: At 28% ACoS Option A's cumulative cash is +$0.4M against Japan's +$1.8M, so Japan is still ahead. A overtakes Japan only at ACoS below about 25.9% (2.40 – 0.674 × (x – 25) = 1.8).
- Numerical error: "At 20% growth after 2030, Option A reaches $40M in about 2032" — judge: 26.4 × 1.2² = $38.0M in 2032; $40M is passed in 2033 ($45.6M). At 10% growth it is passed in 2035. Hedged with 'about', but about 5% short.
- Contradiction: "Japan is ahead on cash to 2030 unless ACoS stays at or below the pilot's 28%" vs "Pilot ad cost (28%): –$2.4M; +$0.4M; 2030 ... J · Japan: +1.8" — judge: On the document's own numbers Japan (+$1.8M) is ahead of Option A at 28% ACoS (+$0.4M), so the stated threshold cannot be right.
- Contradiction: "Amazon-led (A) beats retail-led (B) on every board test" vs "Ambition: US revenue in 2030 — A plan ✗ $26.4M; B ✗ $37.6M" — judge: Minor overstatement: both fail the ambition test and B comes closer, so A does not beat B on every test.
