## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Fix promotions, shrink and delivery pricing, not hours: with store exits, the margin reaches 3.0% by FY2028

**Executive summary:**
1. Promotions, shrink and e-commerce caused 87% of the margin fall; wages only 13%
2. Operating income fell 68% while sales grew 6%; the target needs $29.8M more a year
3. Move store hours instead of cutting them, and exit the nine loss-making stores
4. An 8% cut could lose $10.0M of its $14.5M saving to shrink; moving hours saves $4.2M
5. Return promotions to the FY2023 mix outside ValuMart areas, and grow private label
6. Promotion mix caused over two-thirds of the gross-margin fall; the reset adds $9.9M
7. Keep e-commerce but charge for delivery, which wins back $2.8M of a $10.4M loss
8. Each home delivery loses about $12; the fee test lost only 2% of e-commerce sales
9. First: promotions, delivery fees and store hours, which need no capital
10. Five moves add $27.4M a year and lift the margin to 3.0% by FY2028
11. Together the first three cover the FY2026 covenant need; $6.5M of reserves cover most wage risk
