## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Recommendation: enter the US in 2027 through Amazon (Option A), plan for higher ad costs, and add retail only once awareness passes 20%

**Executive summary:**
1. The US is worth entering now. US K-beauty skincare grew 17% a year in 2021–25 to $3.6B, and 41% of it is bought on Amazon. Onsoo's Amazon pilot sold 64,800 units in six months and holds 4.5 stars.
2. For decision in March 2027: Approve an Amazon-led US launch in 2027 (Option A) and decline the 600-door retail launch (Option B); Fund a US peak-cash envelope of $4M, enough for 32% ACoS plus a 25% duty and far below the $18M cap; Adopt the checkpoints: end-2027 review, and a stop trigger if ACoS stays above 32%; Set the retail gate at Amazon sales above $10M and aided awareness of 20% or more (the CEO's draft says 15%); Confirm that 'on the way to $40M' means a credible path, with $25–30M in 2030; Defer the Japan scale-up; it needs its own case for 2028 or later
3. Retail-led (Option B) fails three of the board's tests on its own planning numbers. Peak cash need is $20.8M against an $18M cap, it has not paid back by 2030, and group margin falls to 4.5% in 2027 against an 8% floor. It also assumes the sell-through of brands with more than 20% awareness. Onsoo is at 4–9%, where the median brand sells below the retailer's delisting threshold.
4. Amazon-led (Option A) passes every financial test in the plan case; payback depends on ad cost. Peak cash is $1.9M and cash is back above zero in 2030 (+$2.4M). Payback needs ACoS of 28.6% or less. The plan assumes 25%, the pilot ran at 28% and brands scaling past $5M have a median of 32%. At 32%, A misses payback by $2.3M but stays far inside the cash cap and the margin floor.
5. Japan scale-up (Option J) is safer but small. It adds $7M of revenue by 2030 in a market growing 1.5% a year, and does nothing for the US ambition.
6. No route reaches $40M by 2030 within the constraints. A reaches $26.4M. B reaches $37.6M, but only by breaking the cash cap, payback and margin floor. We suggest treating $40M as a 2031–32 goal.

- Method
- Volumes, prices, per-unit costs and fixed costs are taken directly from the planning team's Tables 4–7. No outside data was used.
- Amazon contribution per unit = $24.00 − 15% referral − $5.10 FBA − $3.50 product − $0.55 freight − 15% duty on ex-factory − ACoS × price, which gives $4.73 at 25% ACoS.
- Retail contribution per unit = $14.00 wholesale − product − $0.75 freight − duty − 8% co-op − 4% returns = $7.55. Retail units = doors × 4.5 × 52.
- Cash flow = contribution − fixed and one-time costs − increase in inventory. Inventory is four months of landed cost (product + freight + duty), as the planning team specifies.
- Group margin = (non-US plan at 11.0% + US or Japan operating result) ÷ (non-US revenue + added revenue).
- Judgment calls (no one was available to ask)
