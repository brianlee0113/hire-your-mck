## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Fix promotions, shrink and delivery, not wages: six moves reach a 3.2% margin by FY2028

**Executive summary:**
1. Margin fell mainly through promotions, shrink and e-commerce, not wages
2. – Gross margin and e-commerce explain nearly four-fifths of the fall from 3.4% to 1.0%
3. – Hourly pay rose 9.4%, but fewer hours per sale absorbed about 70% of it
4. Promotions, shrink and private label can win back about $14M of gross margin a year
5. – 38% of promotions lost money; returning to the FY2024 share adds about $5.9M
6. – Fixing shrink in the least-staffed stores adds about $3.8M
7. E-commerce loses about $10M a year; repricing home delivery adds $2.8M
8. – Each home delivery loses about $12 after the fee; the fee test kept 98% of online sales
9. Targeted labor cuts and nine store exits save about $14M a year
10. – The proposed 8% cut risks about $7.5M of its $14.5M saving in extra shrink
11. – Six stores close at lease end; three repay their $7.5M exit in 3.4 years
12. Promotions, delivery fees and labor go first, reaching about 3.2% by FY2028
13. – Under a third of the first wave's $16M in FY2026 clears the $20M covenant
14. – Stretch levers worth $8.4M cover a 2027 wage rise of up to $7.3M
