## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Raise mid-market and enterprise prices in 2027, and hold SMB until the promotion cohort is fixed and a price test passes

**Executive summary:**
1. Churn is rising mainly in the 2025 SMB promotion cohort, not in the core customer base
2. Promotion customers explain about 91% of the rise in SMB churn
3. Onboarding is unlikely to cut SMB churn by much more than a third
4. Mid-market can likely take 10% and enterprise 7%, but SMB faces Inkwell's cheaper plan
5. A 10% higher mid-market price converted at 23.1%, against 23.6% (2024 test)
6. Our SMB win rate against Inkwell fell from 51% to 38% in a year
7. A targeted rise adds about $4.2M; 12% on every plan adds about $6.7M, not $10.3M, at more risk
8. Only 76% of ARR renews in 2027, so the CFO's $10.3M cannot all arrive
9. If retention holds, new customers need to add only about $0.8M to hit 15%
10. Decide in December, raise mid-market and enterprise from January, and test SMB first
11. Keep the 2,100 promotion customers out of the rise
12. Stop the SMB rollout if extra churn nears twice the 2023 response
