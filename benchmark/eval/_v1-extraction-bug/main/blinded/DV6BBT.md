## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Launch Copilot in Q2 2027 in a Pro plan at $12 more per seat, not a $149 add-on, and plan on about $2.9M of new ARR

**Executive summary:**
1. Customers value Copilot, but a $149 add-on would bring about $2.5M of new ARR, not $8M
2. Beta productivity rose 9 percentage points more than in matched accounts
3. Only 14% bought at $149, against 46% 'likely' in the survey
4. Routewise has put AI in its plan, but matching it protects at most about $0.6M of new ARR a year
5. Win rate against Routewise fell from 44% to 37%, alongside a 20% discount
6. A Pro plan at $12 more per seat lifts NRR past 105% in its migration year and keeps margin above 75%
7. Pro needs about 26% of accounts to move; 33% moved to the 2023 tier
8. Copilot free in Core would cut gross margin to about 71.9%
9. Launching first is worth the two-quarter mobile delay, narrowly: mobile is the bigger churn problem
10. Mobile reliability is cited in 4.5 times as many churn cases as AI
11. The delay risks up to $1.1M of ARR; waiting defers $1.44M of Pro revenue
12. Launch Pro in Q2 2027 with a usage allowance, restart mobile in Q3 and review at six months
13. Cap use at about 444 requests per seat a month, with paid overage
