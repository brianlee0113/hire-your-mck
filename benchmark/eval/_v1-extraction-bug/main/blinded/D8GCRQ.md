## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Launch Copilot in Q2 2027 inside a Pro tier at $12 more per seat, not as a $149 add-on

**Executive summary:**
1. Copilot is worth launching for productivity; its retention and win-back effects are small
2. Beta accounts scheduled 11% more jobs per dispatcher-hour, against 2% for matched ones
3. AI drives only 6% of churn, and the Routewise win-back is worth at most $0.6M a year
4. Package it in a Pro tier at $12 more per seat, not as a $149 add-on or a free feature
5. Only 14% of offered beta accounts bought at $149, against 46% stated intent
6. Pro at 33% take-up adds about $2.9M; it beats the add-on's best case above 28%
7. Pro meets both financial targets if a usage allowance caps the heaviest accounts
8. NRR rises about 5 percentage points if 26% of accounts take Pro; margin holds near 76%
9. Launch in Q2 2027 for the NRR deadline, and hold the mobile delay to two quarters
10. Delaying mobile loses more ARR for good if the rewrite would keep over 34% of mobile churners
11. Test Pro on non-beta accounts in Q1 2027, launch in Q2 and manage five measures
12. Hold Pro take-up at 26% or more and margin at 75% or more; restart mobile in Q3
