## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Launch Copilot in Q4 2027, after the mobile rewrite, in a $12-a-seat Pro plan, not a $149 add-on

**Executive summary:**
1. Customers value Copilot, but only about one in seven will pay for it as an add-on
2. – Beta volunteers gained 9 percentage points more output per dispatcher-hour than matched accounts
3. – Only 14% of beta accounts paid $149 a month, against 46% stated intent
4. A Pro plan at $12 more per seat with a request allowance beats the add-on within the margin floor
5. – The add-on yields about $0.85M of gross profit a year, not $8M of ARR
6. – Pro adds $2.9M of ARR and reaches 2.4 times as many accounts
7. Routewise's AI is a smaller threat than claimed; mobile reliability loses far more customers
8. – Even the whole win-rate fall is worth at most $0.64M of ARR a year
9. – Mobile reliability drives 27% of churn, against 6% for AI features
10. Shipping the mobile rewrite first protects about twice the ARR that waiting on Copilot risks
11. – A mobile slip risks up to $1.09M of ARR, against $0.56M for Copilot
12. – A Q4 2027 Pro launch still closes the NRR gap if 26% of accounts move
13. Approve Pro now, test its price, and launch in Q4 2027 unless the win rate says AI cannot wait
14. – A win rate still near 37% after the discount would bring the launch forward
