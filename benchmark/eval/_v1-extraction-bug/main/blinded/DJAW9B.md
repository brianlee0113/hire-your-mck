## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Onsoo should enter the US in 2027 through Amazon, not national retail, and fund it step by step against hard gates

**Executive summary:**
1. Answer: yes, enter the US in 2027 with the Amazon-led route (Option A), run as a gated programme. It is the only route that stays inside the board's cash and margin guardrails. Retail-led breaks them on its own numbers, and a Japan scale-up is safe but builds no US business.
2. Why the US: The US is worth entering now: the category is growing fast and Onsoo's products already sell there. US K-beauty skincare grew 17% a year to $3.6B (2021–25), five times faster than the rest of facial skincare. In the Amazon pilot, monthly units nearly doubled in six months (7,400 → 14,000) at 4.5 stars, and 90-day repeat (29%) is close to the 31% benchmark.
3. Which route: Amazon-led is the only route that fits the guardrails; retail-led fails all three financial tests. Option A's cumulative cash bottoms at −$1.9M to −$3.9M against the $18M cap, and group margin stays at 9.0% or more. Option B bottoms at −$20.8M even on its own assumptions, cuts group margin to 4.5% in 2027, and rests on sell-through and halo assumptions the evidence contradicts. Japan adds just $7M of revenue by 2030.
4. Risks and ambition: The binding risk is ad cost: payback by 2030 needs ACoS of 28.6% or less. No route reaches $40M by 2030. The pilot ran at 28% ACoS, but brands scaling from $5M to $20M have a median of 32%. At 32%, Option A is still −$2.3M at end-2030 and payback slips past 2030. The worst tested case costs $6.4M, about a third of the cap. Option A reaches $26.4M in 2030 and about $40M by 2032.
5. What to decide: Approve Option A with hard gates, re-phase the ambition, and replace the 2029 retail trigger. Fund a $4M peak-cash envelope and review at end-2027 against ACoS ≤28%, revenue ≥$7M and repeat ≥31%. Consider retail only when aided awareness is above 20% and a right-sized plan fits the guardrails. A national retail launch in 2029 would cut group margin to 6.0%.
