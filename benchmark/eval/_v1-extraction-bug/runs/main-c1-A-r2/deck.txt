=== Slide 2 ===
[Title] Recommendation: enter the US in 2027 through Amazon (Option A); turn down the 600-door retail launch and hold the Japan scale-up
- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
- Enter the US in 2027
- US K-beauty skincare is $3.6B and has grown 17% a year since 2021, against 5% for all facial skincare. 41% of it sells on Amazon, where Onsoo's six-month pilot already sells 14,000 units a month at 4.5 stars.
- Lead with Amazon (A)
- On the planning team's own inputs, A's cumulative cash bottoms out at −$1.9M, recovers to $2.4M by 2030, and keeps group margin at 9.5% or above. It is the only US route that passes the board's money tests.
- Reject retail-led (B)
- B burns $20.8M by 2030 and never pays back. It also cuts group margin to 4.5% in 2027. Its two key inputs do not hold: it assumes 4.5 units per door per week where brands at Onsoo's awareness sell 2.6, and its 1.3× Amazon "halo" rests on a correlation.
- Hold Japan (J)
- J is safe but small: $7M of extra revenue and $1.8M of cash by 2030, in a market growing 1.5% a year. Management can run only one launch in 2027, and the US is the larger prize.
- Accept two trade-offs
- No route within the cash limits reaches $40M by 2030: A reaches $26M, which points to about 2032 for $40M. And payback by 2030 holds only if Amazon ad cost stays at or below ~28.5% of sales. The plan assumes 25%, the pilot ran at 28%, and scaling brands run at 32%.
- Decide in March
- Approve A with a $4M cash envelope (well inside the $18M cap) and an ad-cost guardrail. Set the retail trigger at 20% aided awareness, not 15%, and restate the US ambition as $40M by 2032.
- Source: Onsoo briefing pack (fictional benchmark data); team analysis.

=== Slide 21 ===
[Title] Method, and the judgement calls made in preparing this document
- APPENDIX A3
- $40M ambition
- Read as a 2030 US revenue target. If the board meant "a trajectory toward $40M", A's $26M growing 29% a year may already satisfy it.
- Revenue basis
- Retail revenue counted at wholesale ($14), the price Onsoo receives; Amazon at $24. Shelf-price revenue would overstate B.
- Cash definition
- Pre-tax, undiscounted; operating result less inventory build (four months of landed cost). No receivables or payables, which would make B worse (retailer terms).
- Doors and fixtures
- Doors sell for the full year they are listed; fixtures paid in the year a door opens. Delisting after 12 months is not modelled, which flatters B.
- Tariff stress
- 25% duty applied to all four years, although the decision is due mid-2027; conservative for both options.
- Group margin
- Ex-US plan (11.0% margin) plus the option's revenue and operating result. Japan option used exactly as given in Table 7.
- Gates and envelope
- The $4M envelope, ACoS guardrails and 20% awareness gate are our proposals, derived from the pack's benchmarks.
- Planning volumes
- A and B volumes used as given. The pilot supports 2027 (1.8× the June run-rate with seven SKUs instead of three); later years are untested.
- Format
- Charts are drawn as native shapes, so they can be edited but are not linked to data. Model figures can be regenerated from the build script.
- All inputs from the briefing pack; no outside data used. Where the pack was silent or ambiguous, the choices listed here were made; all are open to challenge.
