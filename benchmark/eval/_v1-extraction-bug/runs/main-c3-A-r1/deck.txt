=== Slide 2 ===
[Title] Launch Copilot, but in a per-seat Pro tier, and after the mobile rewrite's next two quarters rather than as a $149 add-on in Q2
- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
- Recommendation: launch Dispatch Copilot inside a new Pro plan at $91 per seat (Core + $12) with a pooled AI allowance, targeting Q4 2027. Keep the mobile team intact through Q2 and use H1 to run a paid pilot on randomly chosen accounts.
- The product works for engaged accounts
- Beta accounts scheduled 11% more jobs per dispatcher-hour (matched accounts: +2%) and renewed at 97% vs 93%. All 240 were volunteers, so the gain is not yet proven for the wider base.
- The prize is $1.2–2.9M of new ARR, not $8M
- The 46% "likely to buy" came from the 6% of admins who replied, at larger accounts. When beta accounts were actually asked to pay $149 a month, 14% did.
- Price per seat, inside Pro, with a usage pool
- A flat $149 add-on attracts the heaviest users: buyers' AI cost is $61 a month. Pro at +$12/seat with 600 requests per seat matches Routewise and holds gross margin at 75% or more. Giving Copilot away in Core would cut margin to ~70%.
- Protect mobile first: it is the bigger churn driver
- Mobile reliability is the main reason for 27% of churn; AI for 6%. A two-quarter mobile delay loses an estimated $0.5–1.1M ARR for good. A two-quarter Copilot delay puts at most ~$0.6M at risk.
- 105% NRR needs both levers
- Pro lifts NRR by 2.5–5.0 points once, as accounts migrate. Fixing mobile churn adds 1.9–3.8 points every year. Only the two together reach 105% in the low case.
- Bring launch forward to Q2 if the CTO can staff Copilot without the mobile team, or if the Q4 2026 win rate against Routewise fell below 37% with no Routewise discount running.
- Source: Brightwork briefing pack (synthetic benchmark data); team analysis. Ranges: low case = half the observed rate, high case = observed rate.
