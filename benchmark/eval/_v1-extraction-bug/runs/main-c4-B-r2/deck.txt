=== Slide 2 ===
[Title] Yes, raise prices in 2027, but target them: +10% for mid-market, up to contract caps for enterprise, and hold SMB until a renewal test proves it can bear an increase
- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
- Rising churn is a promotion problem, not a price problem
- •  About 94% of the 1.1-pt rise in SMB monthly churn comes from the 2,100 customers acquired by the 2025 50%-off promotion; they churn ~4.9%/month
- •  Elsewhere churn barely moved: non-promo SMB 2.0→2.1%, enterprise flat. 18% of non-promo churners cite price, vs 19% of all SMB churners in 2025
- •  Onboarding will help but cannot halve SMB churn: even full activation gets it to ~2.0%, not 1.55%
- 1
- 2
- There is headroom in mid-market and enterprise, but it is unproven in SMB
- •  Mid-market: a +10% list test cut conversion by only 0.5 pts (not significant); NRR is 112%; Inkwell is not seen in deals above 200 seats
- •  Enterprise: NRR 118%, churn 0.2%/month, but only 40% of ARR renews in 2027 and 60% is capped at +7%
- •  SMB competes with Inkwell's $9 plan (win rate 51%→38%). An increase breaks even if churn reacts at ~2× the 2023 rate
- 3
- A targeted increase carries far less risk than a blanket 12%, but no pricing option delivers 15% growth on its own
- •  The proposed +12% on every plan is worth $7.6M gross, not $10.3M, because only part of the ARR renews and caps apply. Net of churn it is $1.5–4.6M, and it loses money on SMB in the stress case
- •  The targeted plan nets $2.2–3.6M. Raising prices for the promotion cohort loses money even at the 2023 churn response
- •  With the plan, ARR reaches $96.4–97.8M before new customers. Reaching $98.9M needs $1.1–2.5M of new-logo ARR. NRR stays at ~112–114%
- 4
- Roll out in three gated stages, with triggers that would change the plan
- •  January: reprice mid-market and enterprise renewals. Q1–Q2: randomised SMB renewal test. July: SMB go/no-go. Throughout: an activation programme for the promotion cohort
- •  Watch mid-market renewal churn (>1.0%/month triggers a cap at +5%) and the SMB test arm. Define one board churn metric and get a 2027 new-logo plan
- Source: Palisade Docs briefing pack (synthetic data); team analysis. Ranges are base case to stress case; calculations on slides 9–11 and A1.

=== Slide 6 ===
[Title] An activation programme is worth running but cannot halve SMB churn: lifting the cohort to normal activation cuts SMB churn from 3.1% to ~2.8%, not 1.55%
- 1 · WHY CHURN IS RISING
- How much of the cohort's churn can activation explain? (monthly churn, %)
- Implications
[Table]
  | Step | Calculation | Result |
  | Churn of an activated SMB customer | 2.1% ÷ (72% × 1 + 28% × 3) | 1.35% |
  | Churn of a low-activation customer | 3 × 1.35% | 4.04% |
  | Cohort churn explained by its 41% activation | 1.35% × (41% + 59% × 3) | 2.93% |
  | Excess not explained (discount cliff, low intent) | 4.91% − 2.93% | 1.97% |
  | Cohort churn if activation reaches 72% | 4.91% − (2.93% − 2.10%) | 4.07% |
  | SMB churn after the programme | (3,800 × 2.1% + 2,100 × 4.07%) ÷ 5,900 | 2.8% |
  | Upper bound: every SMB customer activated | All at 1.35%, cohort keeps its cliff excess | 2.0% |
- •  Halving to 1.55% is not credible. It would put SMB below its pre-promotion level (2.0%) and below the pre-increase level in 2023 (1.8%). Even the upper bound only reaches ~2.0%.
- •  2027 ARR effect is small: about $0.27M if the cohort reaches normal activation from January. We include $0.2M on the assumption the programme partly succeeds.
- •  The bigger value is prevention. The cohort shows the cost of a deep, activation-blind discount: 41% activation and ~45% annual churn. Future acquisition offers should reward set-up (for example, documents sent), not just sign-up.
- •  Price policy for the cohort: hold at the current full price through 2027. The cohort cites price 2.9× as often as other churners, so a 12% rise on it loses money even at the 2023 churn response (slide 9).
- Source: Briefing pack §6, §9, §10. Assumes the 3× churn ratio for low activators (<5 documents in month 1) applies across the base, and that the cohort's activation gap explains part of its excess churn. The rest is treated as the effect of the discount ending.

[Title] In SMB, an increase pays only if churn reacts at less than ~2× its 2023 response, and Inkwell makes that uncertain: test before committing
- 2 · WHERE PRICE CAN GO
- Net 2027 ARR from +8% on non-promo SMB renewals, $M
- Why the SMB call is a judgment, not a calculation

response); 1.5×; 2×; 3×

- •  For an increase: the 2023 +8% kept about half its value in year one (1.08 × (1 − 3.6%) = +4.1%). Non-promo churn (2.0→2.1%) and price citations (19%→18%) barely moved from H1 2025 to H1 2026, despite Inkwell’s Oct 2025 launch.
- •  Against: Inkwell Starter costs $9 vs Palisade SMB at $15. A 12% rise widens the gap from 67% to 87%. The SMB win rate where Inkwell is considered has already fallen from 51% to 38%.
- •  Break-even: +8% stops paying if churn reacts at more than 2.1× the 2023 response (+12%: 2.0×). Best case is +$0.7M, which is small against a $16.6M base.

- •  Our call: run a randomised +8% test on non-promo renewals in Q1–Q2 and keep the new-customer list price at $15. Roll out from July only if churn stays within the 2023 pattern. This gives up ~$0.3M of best-case upside to cap the downside.

=== Slide 13 ===
[Title] Roll out in three stages with explicit go/no-go gates, so each step is backed by evidence from the one before
- 4 · HOW TO EXECUTE
- Dec 2026
- Q1 2027
- Q2 2027
- H2 2027
- Mid-market & enterprise:
- Approve +10% / cap policy; brief CS and account teams; renewal notices (90 days)
- Reprice renewals from 1 Jan; track renewal rate weekly
- Gate 1: renewal churn ≤ 1.0%/month and NRR on track → continue
- Continue; prepare 2028 enterprise renewals (60% of ARR)
- SMB non-promo:
- Design randomised test: +8% vs control on renewals; new-customer list stays $15
- Test live; read early churn and exit reasons monthly
- Gate 2 (end June): extra churn ≤ 0.5 pt (2023 pattern) → go
- If go: +8% on all renewals from July. If no-go: stop; keep the test arm
- SMB promo cohort:
- Freeze price; launch activation programme
- Activation outreach; track documents sent
- Measure activation lift vs 41% baseline
- Review whether the cohort joins the 2028 price plan
- Enablers:
- Define the board churn metric
- Monthly pricing dashboard by segment and cohort
- Inkwell win-rate tracking (SMB and MM ≤200 seats)
- Year-end review of elasticity for the 2028 plan
- Source: Team proposal. The SMB test arms are ~1,900 non-promo customers each. Over six months a 0.5-pt monthly churn difference (≈240 vs ≈300 churners) is detectable at roughly 95% confidence.

[Title] How the option values are calculated: price uplift on 2027 renewing ARR minus the churn the price rise triggers
- APPENDIX A1 · MODEL
[Table]
  | Parameter | Value | Basis |
  | Renewing ARR in 2027 | SMB 100% ($21.0M); MM 85% ($34.9M); Ent 40% ($9.6M) | Pack §8 |

  | Gross uplift | Renewing ARR × price % | e.g. MM 34.85 × 10% = $3.49M |
  | Price-driven churn | Renewing ARR × (1 + price) × k × price pts | Lost ARR measured at the new price |
  | k, SMB non-promo | 0.45% per pt (base); 0.90% (stress, 2×) | 2023: (0.5 × 6 + 0.1 × 6) ÷ 8; stress reflects Inkwell |

  | k, mid-market & enterprise | 0.21% per pt (base); 0.45% (stress) | 2024 test: 1 − 23.1/23.6 = 2.1% per 10 pts; stress uses SMB 2023 |
