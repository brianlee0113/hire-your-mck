## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Fix promotions, store routines and delivery fees and close nine weak stores, not cut hours across the board: about 3.2% by FY2028

**Executive summary:**
1. The margin fell from 3.4% to 1.0% through gross margin and e-commerce, not mainly wages – Gross margin and e-commerce caused 79% of the fall; the 3.0% target needs about $30M more a year (p. 5–7)
2. Dropping money-losing promotions adds up to $10.5M a year and private label $4.1M more – 38% of FY2025 promotion events lost money; deeper promotions have not stopped losses near ValuMart (p. 9–15)
3. Fix routines where hours are fewest and trim hours only where most: about $7.0M, not an 8% cut – Store labor caused 13% of the fall; a chain-wide cut could lose up to two-thirds to shrink (p. 17–23)
4. Repricing delivery recovers $2.5M of a $10.4M online loss; closing nine stores adds $6.6M – The average home delivery loses about $12; six of the nine stores can close at lease end (p. 25–33)
5. Seven actions reach about 3.2% for $7.5M one-time if promotions hold; quick wins go first – The 2027 wage rise, up to $7.3M a year, would cut that to 2.7%, so reserve levers are needed (p. 35–40)
