## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Raise prices in 2027 where customers stay for the product, and test SMB before touching it

**Executive summary:**
1. Rising churn comes from the 2025 promotion, not from core customers leaving over price
2. Mid-market and enterprise are the safer place for a 10% rise at renewal
3. An SMB rise is a bet on Inkwell: it breaks even at twice the 2023 reaction
4. The targeted rise adds about $4.3M and keeps the 15% target in reach
5. Reprice where customers stay in January, and test SMB in Q1
