## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Enter the US in 2027 through Amazon, reject the 600-door retail launch, and hold Japan for later

**Executive summary:**
1. The opportunity is real and it is on Amazon.
2. US K-beauty facial skincare reached $3.6B in 2025, growing 17% a year against 5% for the category; 41% of it sells on Amazon. Onsoo's cross-border pilot was running at about $4M a year by June 2026 on three SKUs, rated 4.5 stars.
3. Amazon-led (A) is the only US route that passes the board's cash and margin tests.
4. On planning inputs, A needs at most $1.9M of cash (the cap is $18M), pays back in 2030 (+$2.4M cumulative) and keeps group margin above 9% every year.
5. Retail-led (B) fails on its own numbers, and its inputs are optimistic.
6. Cumulative cash reaches –$18.9M in 2029 and –$20.8M in 2030 with no payback; group margin falls to 4.5% in 2027. At Onsoo's under-10% awareness, newcomers sell 2.6 units per door per week, not the 4.5 assumed and below the 4.0 delisting line. Corrected, B needs about $26M of cash.
7. A's payback is fragile, but its downside is small.
8. Payback by 2030 needs advertising cost of sales (ACoS) at or below 28.6% (plan 25%, pilot 28%, median brand at this scale 32%) and volumes near plan (at 80% of plan it only just pays back). At 32% ACoS A misses payback (–$2.3M at end-2030), but the worst combined case we modelled peaks at $4.6M and the margin floor always holds.
9. No route reaches $40M by 2030.
10. A reaches $26M in 2030. B's $38M rests on the inflated inputs; corrected it is about $28M. $40M should be the goal of a gated second phase in specialty retail, which comes back to the board as its own investment case.
11. Decisions requested in March 2027: (1) approve Option A as Onsoo's single 2027 launch; (2) approve a US cash envelope of $5M through 2030, enough for 80% of plan volume with 32% ACoS and a 25% duty (–$4.6M peak), with anything beyond returning to the board; (3) decline the 600-door retail launch; (4) adopt tighter gates for specialty retail, including aided awareness above 20%, not 15%; (5) re-base the plan at 30–32% ACoS rather than 25%.

=== Slide 18 ===
[Title] How we built the numbers, and the choices we made where the pack was silent
- APPENDIX 3 · METHOD AND JUDGEMENT CALLS
- Method
- All inputs are the planning team's, as given in the briefing pack; no outside data is used.
- Per-unit economics of the hero product (Rice Ferment Barrier Cream) are applied to all SKUs, as the planning team does.
- Cash = operating profit minus the change in inventory, which is held at four months of landed cost. No tax, receivables or payables.
- Group margin adds each option's operating profit and revenue to the ex-US plan (revenue $140–158M at an 11.0% margin).
- ACoS breakeven: the ACoS at which A's cumulative cash is exactly zero at end-2030, holding plan volumes.
- Exchange rate held at KRW 1,380 per dollar; a weaker won would lower dollar product costs.
- Judgement calls
- Ambition read as $40M of US revenue in 2030 (Onsoo's own revenue, with retail at wholesale).
- 2027 volumes treated as full-year, as in the planning team's tables.
- One-time costs and door fixtures expensed in the year incurred (this makes the margin test conservative).
- Onsoo placed in the "below 10%" awareness band (4% of all buyers, 9% of K-beauty buyers).
- The bandwidth limit read as applying to 2027 only, so a Japan scale-up can be considered for 2028.
- Proposed trigger levels and the $5M cash envelope are our recommendations, not board-set figures.
- Japan figures used as given (the pack has no Japan working capital).
