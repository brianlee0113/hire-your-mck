## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Do not buy RouteSprout on the proposed terms in 2027: partner now, and keep a conditional option to buy later at no more than $8.5M upfront

**Executive summary:**
1. Neither acquisition path stays inside the cash limits once 18 months of operating losses are counted
2. Path A needs $22.0M against a $15.0M ceiling; Path B fits at signing but falls to $7.2M–8.2M, below the $9.0M floor
3. Nothing in the plan funds the $7.0M earnout due in 2029
4. The value case is overstated, and buying takes more than 15 years to pay back
5. $18.0M is 3.75 times recognized ARR, not the sellers' 2.5 times bookings
6. The $2.4M cross-sell is ARR, not EBITDA: net it adds about $0.1M a year, and loses $1.2M in the low case
7. Customer and execution risk is high and is not priced into the deal case
8. Churn rose from 12% to 21%, 41 accounts hold 58% of ARR and 30% of integration is unscoped, yet sellers assume 100% renewal
9. Only the partnership passes all four board criteria in 2027; building is affordable but late
10. At most $1.2M at risk, live this summer, no migration, and it out-earns ownership below about $3.5M of ARR
11. Buying stays possible, but only on conditional terms tested at a Q1 2028 gate
12. Partner now with first refusal; buy later for at most $8.5M upfront plus up to $9.5M if earned
13. Buy only if six thresholds clear; integrate via the API and pay nothing that breaks the cash floor
