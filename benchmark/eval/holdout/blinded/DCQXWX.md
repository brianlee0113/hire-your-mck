**Executive summary:**
1. Closing East does not pay once routing and handling are counted
2. The CFO's $1.3M is East's gross fixed cost. Serving 200k Eastern orders from Central adds $1.0M of transport (200k × $5) and $0.2M of damage: a net $0.10M a year, zero at 2028 volume, before the $0.75M exit fee

4. Optimized retention (K) is the cheapest design in the base case: $8.44M in 2027, $0.20–0.45M a year below either closure
5. Closure also breaks, or cannot yet prove, the board's binding constraints
6. Close to Central: 90.3% network two-day rate vs the 92% contract; Central at 100% of capacity in 2027 and 108% in 2028; the $1.1M retrofit it needs is unbudgeted and would take one-time spend to $1.85M vs $0.9M authorized
