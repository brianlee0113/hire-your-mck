## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Keep buying in 2027 on a de-risked supply, pre-qualify a gated lease, and build no cell in 2027

**Executive summary:**
1. Judged on four tests, not unit price, only buying with a de-risked supply fails none – Our starting hypothesis, a leased cell, failed three of the five conditions it needed
2. The owned cell is not a 2027 option – It needs $7.3M against the $5.0M capital limit and leaves ~$3.2M of cash, below the $4.0M floor – The circulated $27 saving is $16 per good housing in 2027; payback is 3.7 years at 100k units (7.2 at 65k)
3. Leasing is a cost near-tie that turns negative if any one assumption slips – It saves $0.3M (1%) over 2027–29 in the plan, but costs $1.4M more on conservative demand – It costs $0.5M more if it runs 7 months of 2027 on full fixed cost; break-even is 207k units in 2028–29
4. Delivery reliability is a supply-design problem that buying can fix faster than any cell – The 0.2-percentage-point shortfall (96.8% on time against 97%) traces to one flood closure – Four supply levers act in 0–13 weeks, against 5–9+ months for a cell, for $0.25M capital and ≤ $0.72M working capital
5. Pre-agreed gates would switch to the lease; act before the March 2027 quote expiry – Triggers: committed 2028–29 volume of 208k+, a price above ~$84 (~$74 with committed volume) or on-time delivery under 97% for two quarters – The plan keeps 2027 year-end cash near $8.2M, enough to fund the lease if a gate trips

[Title] Backup: Each cell's cost per good unit is its cost per start grossed up for scrap, plus its fixed and upfront cost

- Inputs used come straight from the briefing pack

- Worked examples show how each figure is built
- ▪	Buy: $73 + $2 freight/QA per good unit
- ▪	Lease: $49 per start; scrap 6% in year 1, 4% later; $1.6M a year; $2.0M upfront
- ▪	Own: $46 per start; scrap 8% in year 1, 4% later; $0.75M a year; $7.3M upfront; about $2.1M resale after 2029
- ▪	Volumes: plan 80k / 100k / 120k; conservative 80k / 65k / 85k good units
- ▪	Lease 2027: 80,000 ÷ (1 − 6%) = 85,106 starts × $49 = $4.17M + $1.60M fixed = $5.77M
- ▪	Own 2028 (plan): 100,000 ÷ (1 − 4%) = 104,167 starts × $46 = $4.79M + $0.75M fixed = $5.54M
- ▪	Year-end 2027 cash, own: $9.2M − ($7.3M + $4.75M − $6.00M) = $3.15M; lease: $9.2M − ($2.0M + $5.77M − $6.00M) = $7.43M
- ▪	Lease break-even volume: ($2.0M + 2 × $1.6M − $0.23M 2027 saving) ÷ ($75 − $51.04) = 207k units in 2028–29
- ▪	Break-even supplier price: ($22.20M − $6.00M) ÷ 220k = $73.6 (plan); ($18.63M − $6.00M) ÷ 150k = $84.2 (conservative)
