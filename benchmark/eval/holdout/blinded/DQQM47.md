**Governing thought:** Reserve the slot as a capped $0.6M option, bridge 2028 with the contract molder, and order the line only once signed demand and funding clear set triggers

**Executive summary:**
1. The gap is real and starts in 2028, but its size hinges on unsigned demand
2. On accepted units the line is 92% used in 2027, not 83%; overflow starts in 2028 (0.6M)
3. Overflow hits 3.5M units by 2030 at forecast, 0.9M at signed minimums; 2.6M is unsigned

13. Funding: $3.5M+ committed, so cash after the $4.2M payment stays at $7.5M or more
