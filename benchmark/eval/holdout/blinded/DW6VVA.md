## Part 1 · Normalised storyline

**Governing thought:** Do not buy RouteSprout on the proposed terms in 2027. Partner now, and keep a conditional option to buy later at no more than $8.5M upfront

**Executive summary:**
1. Recommendation: Reject the $18.0M all-cash deal (Path A) and the staged deal as structured (Path B). Sign a two-year RouteSprout routing partnership ($1.2M minimum, live summer 2027) that includes a right of first refusal and a pre-agreed conditional purchase option. Exercise it at a Q1 2028 gate only if six evidence thresholds are met.
2. 1 Cash: Neither acquisition path stays inside the cash limits. Path A needs $22.0M against a $15.0M ceiling. Path B fits at signing ($14.5M), but 18 months of operating drag takes cash to $7.2–8.2M, below the $9.0M floor. Nothing in the plan funds the $7.0M earnout due in 2029.
3. 2 Value: The value case is overstated, and payback takes more than 15 years. $18.0M is 3.75x recognised ARR, not the sellers' 2.5x of bookings. The $2.4M cross-sell is ARR, not EBITDA. After margin, support and RouteSprout's losses it adds about $0.1M a year, and loses $1.2M a year in the low case.
4. 3 Risk: Customer and execution risk is high and not priced in. Logo churn rose from 12% to 21%, and 58% of ARR sits in 41 accounts. The sellers assume 100% renewal. 30% of the integration is unscoped, and the synergy evidence is correlation only.
5. 4 Options: Only the partnership passes all four board criteria in 2027. At most $1.2M is at risk, it is live this summer, and no customers have to migrate. It earns more than ownership at any routing ARR below about $3.5M. Building is affordable but ships no earlier than late 2028.
6. 5 Terms: Buying stays possible, but only on conditional terms. At most $8.5M upfront (about 1.8x verified ARR) and up to $9.5M more if earned, paid only while cash stays above the floor. Integrate through the API first, and keep RouteSprout's interface until its large accounts renew.
