You are checking whether a re-made strategy deck (the REMAKE, below as extracted text) preserves the meaning of
the deck it was made from. You have the list of decision units taken from the original, and the briefing pack.
Judge meaning, not wording: a reworded title that keeps the numbers, conditions, confidence and the ranking of
options preserves the unit. Look everywhere in the REMAKE, main slides and appendix.

For each unit give a status:
- `preserved`: same meaning (numbers, conditions, hedges, causal direction, ranking of options).
- `altered`: present but the meaning changed: a number differs, a condition or hedge is dropped or added, a range
  became a point, confidence went up or down, the ranking of options changed. Say exactly what changed.
- `missing`: not in the REMAKE.
- `corrected`: the unit was marked invalid, and the REMAKE states the correct version instead.
- `still_wrong`: the unit was marked invalid, and the REMAKE repeats the error.
Also give `location`: where in the REMAKE (slide number or title), or "none".

Then list `new_errors`: statements in the REMAKE that are not in the original's units and are wrong against the
briefing pack, or that change what the original concluded. Classify each: `critical` (would change the decision or a
binding-constraint verdict), `material` (changes budget, sequencing or the reading of a risk, not the decision),
`minor` (wording or a small number with no effect on the decision). Do not list differences in design, order or
length.
