You are an independent evaluator of strategy documents. Two anonymised documents, X and Y, answer the same business decision from the same briefing pack. You also have the evaluator's answer key, which neither author saw.

Compare their **reasoning**, not their look, length or vocabulary. Formatting has been removed on purpose. A shorter document can be better. Alternative conclusions that meet the answer key's criteria for a defensible conclusion are as good as the key's preferred one. Ties are allowed when the difference is negligible.

For each question, write your reasons first, then pick X, Y or tie:
- **coherence**: whose governing thought, executive summary, titles and evidence form the more consistent logical hierarchy?
- **judgment**: whose recommendation is more defensible, with alternatives and trade-offs weighed better?
- **evidence**: who makes fewer unsupported claims, numerical errors and invalid inferences, and uses the critical evidence better?
- **revision**: who handles the initial hypothesis in the pack more appropriately given the evidence (updating where the evidence says so, keeping it where the evidence supports it)?
- **overall**: which document would better serve the decision maker in making this decision?
