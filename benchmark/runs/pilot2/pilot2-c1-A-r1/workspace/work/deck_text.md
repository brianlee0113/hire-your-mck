## Slide 2
- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
- Enter the US in 2027 through Amazon, reject the 600-door retail launch, and hold Japan for later
- Source: Onsoo planning-team briefing pack (synthetic benchmark data); team analysis.
- The opportunity is real and it is on Amazon.
- US K-beauty facial skincare reached $3.6B in 2025, growing 17% a year against 5% for the category; 41% of it sells on Amazon. Onsoo's cross-border pilot was running at about $4M a year by June 2026 on three SKUs, rated 4.5 stars.
- Amazon-led (A) is the only US route that passes the board's tests.
- On planning inputs, A needs at most $1.9M of cash (the cap is $18M), pays back in 2030 (+$2.4M cumulative) and keeps group margin at 9.5% or more every year.
- Retail-led (B) fails on its own numbers, and its inputs are optimistic.
- Cumulative cash reaches –$18.9M in 2029 and –$20.8M in 2030 with no payback; group margin falls to 4.5% in 2027. At Onsoo's under-10% awareness, newcomers sell 2.6 units per door per week, not the 4.5 assumed and below the 4.0 delisting line. Corrected, B needs about $26M of cash.
- A's real risk is ad cost, not demand.
- Payback by 2030 holds only while advertising cost of sales (ACoS) stays at or below 28.6%. The plan assumes 25%, the pilot ran at 28%, and brands growing from $5M to $20M a year typically run at 32%. At 32%, A misses payback (–$2.3M at end-2030), but peak cash stays at about $3.2M and the margin floor holds.
- No route reaches $40M by 2030.
- A reaches $26M in 2030. B's $38M rests on the inflated inputs; corrected it is about $28M. $40M should be the goal of a gated second phase in specialty retail, which comes back to the board as its own investment case.
- Decisions requested in March 2027: (1) approve Option A as Onsoo's single 2027 launch; (2) approve a US cash envelope of $4M through 2030, enough for benchmark ad costs plus a 25% duty (–$3.9M peak); (3) decline the 600-door retail launch; (4) adopt tighter gates for specialty retail, including aided awareness above 20%, not 15%; (5) re-base the plan at 30–32% ACoS rather than 25%.
