## Judgement calls made without input
- **$9.5M scope:** the pack does not say whether the $9.5M includes today's $0.4M monthly burn, so both readings are shown. The recommendation holds under either.
- **Runway rule:** tested at today's burn, which is the most favourable test for the plan. The plan's 18-month spending is assumed to be even.
- **Contribution definition:** per-order contribution is built from Table 3 inputs; "after site costs" also deducts dark-store fixed cost ÷ (orders/day × 365).
- **Software path:** illustrative, not a forecast. Burn is taken as operating loss, costs other than the $0.8M hires stay flat, and growth compounds monthly.
- **Option B** (a one- or two-store test of our own) was added as a middle option for comparison.
- **Channel conflict** is marked as the team's judgement; the pack has no direct customer evidence on it.
