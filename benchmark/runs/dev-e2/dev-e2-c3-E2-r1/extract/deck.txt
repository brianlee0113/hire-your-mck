=== Slide 18 ===
[Title] Four gates let the executive team switch package or order before the cost is sunk
- 1 Demand    2 Packaging    3 Margin    4 Timing and gates
- Decision gates for the Dispatch Copilot launch, thresholds from the analysis in this document
[Table]
  |  | Threshold | When checked | If crossed |
  | Pro conversion compared with add-on conversion in a randomized two-offer test | Pro wins fewer than twice as many accounts | End of March 2027, after one build quarter | Launch the $149 add-on in Q2 instead of Pro |
  | Win rate against Routewise in Q4 2026, after its promotion ended | Back to 44% or more | January 2027 planning | Treat the Q3 fall as price-driven; drop win-back from the case |
  | Share of accounts on Pro six months after launch | Below 13%, half the 26% a year the NRR target needs | Q4 2027 | Offer the $149 add-on to Core accounts alongside Pro |
  | Mobile rewrite back at full staffing | Any slip beyond the two quarters planned | Start of Q3 2027 | Pause new Copilot features until the rewrite is staffed |
- If the first gate fails, stopping after one quarter caps the sunk build cost at about $0.74M
- 1  Thresholds: 2.0x = add-on revenue per account ÷ Pro revenue per account (p. 9); 26% = Pro uptake for 4 percentage points of NRR (p. 10)
