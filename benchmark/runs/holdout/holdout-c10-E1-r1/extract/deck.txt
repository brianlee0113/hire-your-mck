=== Slide 11 ===
[Title] The renewal gap of 9 percentage points shows who buys C; it does not prove that C drives customers away
- 1 Economics    2 Exit cost    3 Evidence    4 Plan
- 1
- The claim rests on a snapshot, not a test
- 2
- C buyers differ in ways that predict churn on their own
- 3
- Before acting on it, test it like for like
- ▪	The CEO's scatter plot compares hospital renewal rates by product mix
- ▪	It shows that C accounts renew less often, not why
- ▪	No account in the sample is seen before and after a change in C
- ▪	Smaller hospitals buy C disproportionately
- ▪	C is concentrated in accounts running legacy equipment
- ▪	C accounts get lower customer-success coverage
- ▪	Each of these can lower renewal whether or not C is in the order
- ▪	Compare C and non-C accounts of the same size, equipment and coverage
- ▪	Track renewal where coverage rises but C stays
- ▪	Until then, read the gap as a coverage signal, not a product verdict
- ▪	The evidence neither proves nor rules out a C effect
