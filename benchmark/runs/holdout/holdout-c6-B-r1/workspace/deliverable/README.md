**Deliverable:** `Meridian_Motion_Housing_Make_Lease_Buy.pptx` (18 slides, 16:9). The deck uses only `evidence/briefing-pack.md`.

## Governing thought
Keep buying housings in 2027. Turn the single-supplier arrangement into a dual-sourced supply with reserved capacity and a five-layer outage contingency. Pre-qualify a leased cell that is triggered only by pre-agreed gates. Do not build the owned cell.

## Choices made without being able to ask (also listed in Appendix B)
- **Horizon and basis:** 2027–29, nominal, pre-tax, undiscounted, as the pack instructs. The $2.1M resale is shown separately because it is not binding.
- **Timing:** both cells are assumed to run for all of 2027. That is not physically possible and it favours the cells. Partial-year cases are tested on slide 9.
- **Cash test:** year-end cash = $9.2M minus the 2027 cash outflow above today's $6.0M buy spend. This assumes the rest of the business is cash-neutral. The buy option is charged for its $0.72M buffer stock.
- **Emergency supplier:** costed at $88 plus $2 freight/QA. **Alternate-shop unit price:** assumed equal to the current quote; this needs a quote to confirm.
- **Not used as evidence:** the 98.5% demo yield and the machine-shop correlation, for the reasons given in the pack.
