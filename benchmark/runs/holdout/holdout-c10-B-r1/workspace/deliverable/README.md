# Vesper MedTech: Connector C board deck

**Files**
- `Vesper_MedTech_Connector_C_Board_Deck.pptx`: the 19-slide board deck. It is built from editable native PowerPoint shapes.
- `Vesper_MedTech_Connector_C_Board_Deck_preview.pdf`: a raster preview of the same slides, drawn with Arial metrics. Fonts may differ very slightly from PowerPoint.
- The build script is `../work/build_deck.py`. Run `python3 work/build_deck.py` to regenerate the deck.

**Governing thought:** do not discontinue C in 2027. Its 8% margin comes from the cost allocation, and exiting would cost $4.75M in the first year and $2.65M a year after. Exit would also breach the $0.9M cash envelope and the 95% bundle-fill requirement. Instead, redesign C (+$2.4M over 2027–29), recover bundle fill, then move the team to D, and hold a Q4 2028 gate on any 2029 sunset.

**Judgement calls made without being able to ask** (listed in full on slide 19)
1. The exit takes effect on 1 Jul 2027, and the full $2.1M penalty falls in 2027.
2. The penalty counts against the $0.9M one-time cash envelope.
3. The 25% migration rate is used only as a ceiling.
4. The $2.9M of stranded cost is reallocated pro rata to today's A/B/D allocation (illustrative only).
5. Redesign starts in Mar 2027, with 4 months of validation and a 2-month pilot; savings start in Sep 2027.
6. The $1.25M saving is assumed to reduce C's direct cost (used only for the ~29% reported-margin figure).
7. The time horizon is 2027–29, undiscounted, with FY2026 volumes held flat.
8. The bundle-fill target of Q3 2027 and the workstream leads are proposals for management to confirm.
