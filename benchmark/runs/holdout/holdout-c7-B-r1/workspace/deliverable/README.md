## Answer (governing thought)
Do not buy RouteSprout on the proposed terms in 2027. Neither the $18.0M all-cash deal (A) nor the staged deal as structured (B) passes.

Instead, sign the two-year partnership now. It has a $1.2M minimum and goes live in summer 2027. It should include:
- a right of first refusal on any sale of RouteSprout;
- a pre-agreed, conditional option to buy: at most $8.5M upfront and up to $9.5M earned, paid only while cash stays above the floor.

Decide on exercising the option at a Q1 2028 gate, against six thresholds set in advance.

## Storyline
1. Executive summary and the hypothesis test. The starting hypothesis was to buy on staged terms; it fails four of five tests, and the fifth is unproven.
2. Cash (p.4–5). Path A breaks the $15.0M ceiling. Path B falls to $7.2–8.2M against the $9.0M floor, and the 2029 earnout has no funding source.
3. Value and payback (p.6–8). The price is 3.75x ARR, not 2.5x. Cross-sell contribution is +$0.07M a year (base) or −$1.21M (low), and payback takes more than 15 years.
4. Customer continuity and execution (p.9–10). Churn rose from 12% to 21%, 58% of ARR is in 41 accounts, and the synergy claims rest on correlations.
5. Alternatives (p.11–13). A scorecard against the board's four criteria. The partnership earns more than ownership below about $3.5M of routing ARR. The build ships late 2028 at the earliest.
6. Terms, triggers, next steps and risks (p.14–18), followed by two appendices: the assumptions register and the calculation detail.

## Choices made without being able to ask
- **Timing:** an acquisition would close in July 2027, after about 3 months of diligence. That gives an 18-month window to end-2028.
- **RouteSprout EBITDA** is held at −$1.0M a year. Cross-sell ARR ramps linearly to its end-2028 target.
- **$0.85M a year of integration-era support** is counted for those 18 months and then ends. The pack's $4.0M cap names "customer support", so the deck shows both readings: as a cost against cash, or as part of the cap. The conclusion holds either way.
- **Partnership:** the $1.2M minimum is treated as a fixed cost that cannot be recouped (conservative). Partner-sold routing uses the same $0.8–2.4M ARR range at 80% gross margin, with Atlas taking 55%.
- **The build's "$1.3M of 2028 ARR"** is treated as a 2029 run-rate, because launch can be no earlier than late 2028.
- **One routing strategy:** partnering with RouteSprout in 2027 and buying it in 2028 counts as a single strategy, since it is the same product. If the board disagrees, the 2028 gate becomes a choice between the partnership and the build only.
- **Earnout above the cash floor:** any amount that cannot be paid in cash is deferred or settled in equity. Equity settlement would need shareholder approval, and the deck flags that.
- **Diligence cost** is not in the pack. The deck asks the CFO to size it.
- **No DCF**, as the board instructed. Payback is simple and undiscounted.
