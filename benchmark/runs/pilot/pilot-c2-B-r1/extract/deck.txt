=== Slide 2 ===
[Title] Executive summary: fix promotions, shrink, delivery pricing and loss-making stores rather than cutting store hours, and start with the tested, low-capital moves now
- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
- 1  Wages are not the main cause of the decline
- Operating margin fell 2.35 pts (3.37% → 1.02%, FY2023–25). Gross margin explains 1.02 pts (promotions −0.70, shrink −0.50, partly offset by private label +0.18) and e-commerce fulfillment 0.84 pts. Store labor explains only 0.30 pts, because fewer hours per $ of sales (−6%) absorbed most of the +9.4% wage rise.
- 2  Management’s draft (cut hours 8%, freeze e-commerce) would not reach the target
- The $14.5M saving would probably shrink to ~$4.5M, because thinly staffed stores run 3.1% shrink against 2.3% in the best-staffed. That leaves FY2026 operating income near the $20M covenant and the FY2028 margin at about 1–1.5%. The freeze also locks in an e-commerce loss of $10.4M a year.
- 3  Instead, pull four groups of levers worth ~$29M a year by FY2028
- Commercial (cut loss-making promotions, rebuild vendor funding, grow private label) $13.5M · store operations (cut shrink, reshape rather than cut labor) $6.4M · re-price home delivery, which is kept $2.5M · exit the nine stores with negative four-wall EBITDA $6.6M.
- 4  This protects the covenant but reaches 3.0% only if upside lands
- Base case: FY2026 operating income $24.8M (covenant $20M) and FY2028 margin 2.65%, after absorbing a $6.8M wage step. Reaching 3.0% needs $4.9M more, about one-third of the $15M upside we identified. Capital needed: ~$20M of the $25M envelope.
- 5  What to do first (next 90 days)
- Roll out the tested delivery fee chain-wide. Drop the money-losing promotions and make vendor co-funding a condition of every event. Cancel the blanket hours cut and move hours to produce and receiving. Serve notice on the six expiring leases. Rebid the delivery contract instead of accepting +3%.
- Source: Harlow Fresh Markets briefing pack (synthetic data); team analysis. FY = fiscal year ending January. Base case = risk-adjusted plan; all lever values are annual run-rates.

=== Slide 21 ===
[Title] Appendix: how the key figures were calculated
- APPENDIX
- Margin bridge. Each cost line’s % of sales in FY2025 minus FY2023. Gross profit 26.04% → 25.02% (−1.02 pts); e-commerce 0.37% → 1.20% (+0.84); labor 11.80% → 12.10% (+0.30); occupancy 5.80% → 6.00% (+0.20); other SG&A 4.70% → 4.70%.
- Gross-margin split. Promo: (29.0 − 24.0) × 14 pts = −0.70 pts. Private label: (18.5 − 17.0) × 12 pts = +0.18. Shrink: 2.6 − 2.1 = −0.50. Total −1.02 pts. $ values at FY2025 sales of $1,503M.
- Labor. Hours = 6.60 × 1,503 = 9.92M (FY2025) vs 7.02 × 1,420 = 9.97M (FY2023). Cost = hours × wage (reconciles to $181.9M / $167.6M within rounding).
- Management’s 8% cut. 8% × $181.9M = $14.6M. Shrink effect 8 × 0.083 pt = +0.67 pt × $1,503M = −$10.0M. FY2026 ≈ 15.3 + 14.6 − 10.0 = $19.8M.
- Vendor funding. Net promo give-up 14% × $435.9M = $61.0M = 54% of gross markdowns, so gross ≈ $113M and 1 pt of funding ≈ $1.13M.
- E-commerce. Sales 1,331k × $96 = $127.8M. Incremental contribution 30% × 20% × $127.8M = $7.7M. Net fulfillment $18.1M (picking 1,331k × $7.40 + delivery 772k × $13.80 − fees 772k × $3.10). Net = −$10.4M.
- Fee rollout. Delivered 772k × 0.91 = 703k; click-and-collect 559k × 1.14 = 637k. New net fulfillment $15.1M (saving $3.0M). Lost contribution 2% × $127.8M × 20% = $0.5M. Net +$2.5M.
- Stores. Per store: sales $13.1M, EBITDA −$0.34M. Gain = EBITDA avoided + sales × 25% × 12%. Six: 2.07 + 2.36 = $4.43M; three: 1.03 + 1.18 = $2.21M.
- FY2028 base. Ratio levers × 0.941 (continuing-sales factor). Margin = $37.5M ÷ $1,411.9M sales (FY2025 less $88.5M net closure loss less $2.6M e-commerce) = 2.65%.
- Wage step. 4% × $181.9M × 0.941 = −$6.85M (assumes the rise flows through the whole pay scale).
- Source: Harlow Fresh Markets briefing pack (synthetic data); team analysis.
