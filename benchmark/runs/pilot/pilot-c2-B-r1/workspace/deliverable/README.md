# Harlow Fresh Markets: margin plan for the February 2027 board meeting

**Files**
- `Harlow_margin_plan_board_Feb2027.pptx`: 22-slide board deck (16:9). Charts are native PowerPoint charts, so you can edit them.
- `harlow_margin_model.py`: the model behind every number in the deck. Run it with `python3 harlow_margin_model.py` to print the figures.

**Governing thought:** Harlow should reverse its margin decline by fixing promotions, shrink, delivery pricing and its loss-making stores, not by cutting store hours. It should start with the tested, low-capital moves.

**Storyline (pyramid)**
1. **Why margin fell (slides 4–7):** of the 2.35-pt decline, gross margin explains 1.02 pts (promotions −0.70, shrink −0.50, private label +0.18) and e-commerce fulfillment 0.84 pts. Store labor explains only 0.30 pts. E-commerce loses about $10.4M a year.
2. **What to do (slides 8–13):** four plans compared. The recommended plan has four lever groups worth about $29M a year by FY2028 in the base case: commercial $13.5M, store operations $6.4M, delivery re-pricing $2.5M, and exiting the nine weak stores $6.6M.
3. **What it delivers (slides 14–17):**
   - FY2026 operating income: $24.8M in the base case, against a $20M covenant.
   - FY2028 margin: 2.65% in the base case. Reaching 3.0% needs $4.9M of the $14.7M upside identified.
   - Capital: about $20M of the $25M envelope.
4. **What to do first (slides 18–20):** a roadmap, the risks, and four decisions for the board.

**How the hypothesis changed:** the starting hypothesis was "fix promotions, shrink and delivery, not wages, and reach 3.0%". Testing changed it in three ways:
- All nine loss-making stores moved into the core plan.
- Labor moved from "don't touch" to "reshape": −2% net hours, taken from the most heavily staffed stores.
- 3.0% is shown as a base case of 2.65% plus a named stretch plan, not as a certainty.

**Judgment calls made without input** (slide 22 lists them all):
- FY2026 is treated as the first plan year and the covenant test year.
- The baseline is the FY2025 run-rate.
- The 2027 wage step is applied to the whole store payroll (a conservative choice).
- Promotion savings are realized at 70%.
- Vendor funding is valued at $1.13M per point. This is derived from the pack, not reported in it.
- The nine weak stores are assumed to be equal in size.
- Capital lines other than the $7.5M lease exit are planning allowances.

The only source is `evidence/briefing-pack.md`, which contains synthetic data.
