# Parcelbird Now: board deck

- `Parcelbird-Now-board-deck.pptx`: the deck (14 slides, 16:9). It is fully editable. Charts are drawn as native shapes, not chart objects.
- `Parcelbird-Now-board-deck.pdf`: the same slides as images, for quick reading. It is made from the same layout spec as the PPTX.

**Recommendation:** do not launch Parcelbird Now in 2027. Fund the software business instead: $0.8M a year for 2 engineers and 2 sales hires. Agree now on the conditions that would reopen the question.

## Choices made without asking (also on slide 14)
1. **Two contribution measures.** One is before dark-store fixed costs, the other after them. The pack does not define the founders' or incumbents' "contribution per order".
2. **The $9.5M plan figure.** The pack does not say whether this includes today's $0.4M a month burn, so the deck shows both readings. The plan breaks the 12-month runway rule under either one.
3. **Runway** is cash ÷ current monthly burn. The reserve is tested at today's burn, which is the most lenient test.
4. **The $12.0M cash** is treated as the cash available in January 2027.
5. **The software path** on slide 11 is an illustrative top-down model, with its assumptions stated on the slide. It is not a forecast.
6. **Pop-up exit rate** (~410 orders a day) assumes the 18% weekly growth was steady.
7. **Data inconsistency flagged:** the pack reports month-three retention from a six-week test. The deck asks management to confirm how this was measured.
8. **Option B**, a single-store test, was added as a middle option for the board to weigh.

All figures come from `evidence/briefing-pack.md` or are derived from it. No external research was used.
