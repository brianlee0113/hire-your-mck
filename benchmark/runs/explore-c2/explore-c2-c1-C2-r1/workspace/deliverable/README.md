## Material deliberately not used as headline numbers

- The $5.2B trade-press "US K-beauty market" figure: it includes makeup and hair care. The deck uses $3.6B of facial skincare and explains why on p. 4.
- The 0.71 doors-to-Amazon correlation, used as proof of a 1.3x halo: correlation, not cause (p. 12).
- The 4.5 units per door per week assumption: it comes from successful newcomers only (p. 11).
