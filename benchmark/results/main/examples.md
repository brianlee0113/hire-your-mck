## Substantive reasoning errors in a Hire Your McK deck: c2-profitability, Hire Your McK, replicate 3

Weighted 4.05; 1 numerical errors, 2 contradictions, 7 unsupported claims (primary judge).

- Numerical error: "Cutting hours treats the smallest cause and feeds the second-largest, shrink" — judge: On the document's own bridge, store labor (−0.30 pp) is not the smallest cause (occupancy and energy is −0.20 pp), and shrink (−0.50 pp) is the third-largest cause after e-commerce fulfillment (−0.84 pp) and promotion mix (−0.70 pp).
- Contradiction: "The draft plan could miss the lender's covenant, because it cuts a cost that did not cause the decline" vs "Store labor: a real cost rise, but the third-smallest step... Store labor's share of the fall 13%" — judge: The document's own bridge attributes 13% of the margin fall to store labor, so labor did contribute to the decline.
- Contradiction: "Shrink rises in proportion to hours cut; the cut lands evenly on all stores" vs "$3.6M 8% fewer hours in the best-staffed quarter, once shrink holds... $2.8M shrink down to FY2023's 2.1% of sales instead of 2.3%" — judge: The reserve takes hours out of Q4 stores twice (moved to Q1/Q2, then cut 8%) while assuming shrink falls further to 2.1%. That conflicts with the hours-to-shrink link the document uses to reject the draft plan, though it is gated on shrink holding.
