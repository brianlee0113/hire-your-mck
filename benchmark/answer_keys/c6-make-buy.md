- **K1** **critical**: Owned cell requires $7.30M upfront, above $5.00M capex cap and leaves only $1.90M cash versus $4.00M floor.

- **K5**: Leased-cell three-year total including setup is $22.20M on plan volumes, but its forecast has capacity, contract and downtime risk.
- **K6**: Supplier reliability is 96.8% against a 97% requirement; reserve capacity and build contingency rather than treat make-or-buy as only a cost choice.
