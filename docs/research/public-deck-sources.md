# Public McKinsey slide-deck sources (for layout-grammar study only)

Collected 2026-11-05. Purpose: learn abstract structure (archetypes, action titles, trackers, source lines). Do not redistribute.

**How "Verified" was checked:** `V` means the URL was fetched directly (HTTP 200, real PDF or PPTX), parsed with pypdf, and found to have mostly landscape pages with McKinsey branding or a McKinsey footer in the text. `V*` means the URL returned 200 and a valid file, but the file could not be text-checked: it is encrypted, scanned, or a PPTX. In those cases McKinsey authorship comes from the title page or from public catalogs. Page counts are the parsed counts.

**What "Footer" means:** the deck carries the standard line "CONFIDENTIAL AND PROPRIETARY – Any use of this material without specific permission of McKinsey & Company is strictly prohibited". Every deck here was intentionally published by the host, such as a government agenda packet, a conference proceedings site, or a court or commission archive.

## Search strategies — what worked

1. **Mining aggregator catalogs (best yield).** Deckary and Slideworks already list more than 150 decks with direct host URLs. The work was mostly filtering them: drop Scribd and leaked material, keep intentionally published ones.
2. **Searching for the exact footer string.** "Any use of this material without specific permission of McKinsey & Company is strictly prohibited" or "CONFIDENTIAL AND PROPRIETARY" combined with `.gov` or filetype:pdf surfaced in.gov, wstc.wa.gov, WTO, IACPM and the San Diego EDC. This also works in Japanese: **「機密・専有情報 マッキンゼー・アンド・カンパニーによる個別の明示的な承諾を得ることなく」** combined with go.jp returned three METI/ANRE decks right away. Japanese 審議会 (government council) archives look like a rich, under-mined source.
3. **Conference proceedings sites** (World Materials Forum yearly 2016–2024, EIA, UN, UNCTAD, PIIE, EBF, GSA) host speaker decks openly. The same host often has several years of McKinsey decks.
4. **What did not work:** generic Korean queries (맥킨지 발표자료). They return books and summaries; the Seoul 2013 booklet was the only Korean slide source found. mckinsey.com itself could not be fetched from this environment (timeouts), and several .gov sites (HHS, Vancouver, NZ Treasury, NH) block automated fetches with 403.
